How the five views connect

Local evidence

i(x;y) = log₂[p(x,y) / (p(x)p(y))]. A positive value favors the observed joint over independence; a pointwise value can be negative. Weighting the map shows p(x,y)i(x;y), whose integral is mutual information.

A selected slice

DKL(p(X|y₀) || p(X)) averages the pointwise information along one slice. KL divergence cannot be negative, and one selected slice may exceed the global average.

The global average

I(X;Y) = ∬ p(x,y)i(x;y) dx dy = ∫ p(y) DKL(p(X|y) || p(X)) dy.

The page uses a normalized 384 × 384 cell-centered grid over a finite window. Infinite tails are truncated at six noise standard deviations. Fading and color saturation affect only the display, never the integrals.
